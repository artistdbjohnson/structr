Movement illustrations
======================

The SVG frames in this folder are unmodified copies from Workout Guide
by Bryl Lim (https://bryllim.github.io/workout-guide/).

License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Included, three frames each:

- worlds-greatest-stretch
- childs-pose

The Workout Guide manifest records these frames as original artwork by
Bryl Lim under CC BY-SA 4.0. It does not list a separate Everkinetic
source URL for these two stretches.

No changes were made to the SVG files. They are bundled here so a session
can show them without a CDN. If you adapt them, ShareAlike applies:
keep the adaptation under CC BY-SA 4.0, credit Bryl Lim and Workout Guide,
and say what changed.

Other session moves use Structr's own placeholder cue card. Those
placeholders are not Workout Guide artwork.
