# Free RepDB stills in Structr

Exercise data by [RepDB](https://repdb.co) (repdb.co).

Free tier only. Still WebP files from `https://exercise-dataset.com/images/flat/{id}-start.webp`, `-peak.webp`, or `-main.webp`. Loaded when a How sheet opens. Not vendored. Not the paid loops. Nothing from `premium-samples/`.

License: in-app use with the credit above. Do not redistribute this as a dataset. Do not use the images as generative-AI training or reference. Full terms: [LICENSE-DATA.md](https://github.com/RepDB/exercise-dataset/blob/main/LICENSE-DATA.md).

How sheets keep Structr's own cues. The picture is the still. Start / Peak is a toggle, not an animation.

World's greatest stretch is Bryl Lim, Workout Guide, CC BY-SA 4.0. RepDB has no match.
