## 1. What this is (and why skill practice)

The big three — squat, bench, deadlift — are skills with a short attention budget. Flooding yourself with a 47-point checklist mid-set helps nobody. Structr’s barbell wedge picks **a few high-leverage cues per lift** and practices them inside the same five-phase session shape:

**Warm-up → Skill drills → Form → THE BULK → Cool-down**

This is form practice. The empty bar and the light plates earn their place. More reps on The Bulk come after the shape feels like yours.

**What we ship in this module**

| Template | Skill spine |
|----------|-------------|
| **Squat Basics** | Brace → knees track → depth you own |
| **Bench Basics** | Setup lock → bar to lower chest → stack wrists |
| **Deadlift Basics** | Mid-foot bar → slack out → push the floor |

> If it hurts in a sharp way, rack the bar. Film a set, or find a coach when you can.

## 2. Easy misses

### Squat

**The idea:** A big brace before you sit. Knees travel the way the toes point. Depth you own: hip crease below the top of the knee, when that's honest for you.

**Common misses**

| Mistake | What it looks like | Better cue |
|---------|-------------------|------------|
| Soft brace | Breath leaks mid-descent; low back rounds | Belly breath + brace **before** the sit; hold through the hole |
| Knee cave | Knees dive in out of the hole | Knees track over 2nd/3rd toes; widen stance a touch if needed |
| Cutting depth | Stops high; “feels fine” because the hard part was skipped | Film side/45° — hip crease below knee top |
| Heel rise | Toes take over | Stance width / foot angle first; mobility later |
| Cue overload | Twelve thoughts at once | Fix **one** cue per session |

### Bench press

**The idea:** Feet planted. Upper back pinned. A small arch you can own. Bar to the lower chest. Elbows in enough that the wrists stack over them.

**Common misses**

| Mistake | What it looks like | Better cue |
|---------|-------------------|------------|
| Floppy setup | Feet float; blades unlock | Plant feet; squeeze blades into the bench before unrack |
| High touch | Bar hits upper chest / neck line | Touch near nipple line; slight arc toward the rack |
| Elbow flare | Wrists bent back; shoulders complain | Tuck; stack wrist over elbow at the bottom |
| Bounce | Rebound off the chest | Soft touch / controlled pause when learning |
| Pressing with soft shoulders | Blades slide at the bottom | Keep the set — don’t unlock to “reach” |

### Deadlift

**The idea:** Bar over the middle of your foot, about an inch from the shin. Shoulders a little ahead of the bar. Pull the slack out, then push the floor away.

**Common misses**

| Mistake | What it looks like | Better cue |
|---------|-------------------|------------|
| Bar too far | Bar at toes; swings in as it leaves the floor | Mid-foot every time; eyes closed check |
| Yank | Hips shoot up; back rounds early | Slack out first; then push floor away |
| Rounded start | Soft spine before plates leave | Brace + lat set in the setup — not after |
| Bar drift | Bar leaves the shins | Drag the bar up the legs |
| Squatty pull | Hips too low; turns into a confused squat | Hinge to the bar; hips where arms hang long |

**Film rule (practice culture):** Side angle for deadlift and bench; ~45° for squat. Bad reps are information — fix one thing next set.

## 3. The three plans

### Squat Basics · ~40–50 min

**Purpose:** Own brace, tracking, and depth before chasing numbers.  
**Who it’s for:** New barbell lifters, returners cleaning half-reps, anyone coming from goblet work.  
**Session shape**

| Phase | What you do |
|-------|-------------|
| Warm-up | Hip and ankle openers, empty-bar squat practice |
| Skill drills | Pause squats (light), brace holds, depth checks |
| Form | Working sets — quality over load |
| **THE BULK** | More sets, a weight you can own · **aim for 7–8** |
| Cool-down | Fold, hip openers, breathe |

### Bench Basics · ~40–50 min

**Purpose:** Lock a repeatable setup and a quiet bar path.  
**Who it’s for:** After upper-back set feels natural; people whose shoulders complain from floppy presses.  
**Session shape**

| Phase | What you do |
|-------|-------------|
| Warm-up | Scap sets, light push-ups / empty bar |
| Skill drills | Pause bench (light), wrist-stack drills |
| Form | Working sets with setup checklist |
| **THE BULK** | More bench reps · **aim for 7–8** |
| Cool-down | Slow shoulder circles, a doorway reach, breathe |

### Deadlift Basics · ~40–50 min

**Purpose:** Groove mid-foot setup and a non-yank pull.  
**Who it’s for:** Anyone who yanks the bar off the floor, and anyone coming from kettlebell swings.  
**Session shape**

| Phase | What you do |
|-------|-------------|
| Warm-up | Sit-back practice, a light deadlift with soft knees, brace and breathe |
| Skill drills | Slack-out practice, paused below knee (light) |
| Form | Working singles/doubles — quality |
| **THE BULK** | More pulls, a weight you can own · **aim for 7–8** |
| Cool-down | Hip flexor stretch, easy hamstrings, breathe |

Default open: **Squat Basics** (pairs cleanly with dumbbell Goblet Path as a prior skill).

## 4. How hard it felt

Same 1–10 scale as kettlebell and dumbbell. You mark it on The Bulk. Most days land around **7–8**: hard, and still crisp. Deadlift singles can sit closer to **6–7** when the session says so.

## 5. What you need

| Need | Notes |
|------|-------|
| Bar + plates | Standard Olympic bar when possible; learn empty-bar first |
| Rack / stands | Squat and bench need a rack you trust |
| Flat shoes | Stable sole; save soft runners for elsewhere |
| Optional | Belt later — not day-one mandatory |

Clear space behind the bar for deadlift; collars on. Spotter or pins for heavy bench when loads climb.

## 6. FAQ

**Is this a powerlifting program?**  
These are form-practice sessions. The numbers climb when the reps stay clean.

**Which plan first?**  
**Squat Basics**, unless you’ve already got a solid squat and want bench or pull emphasis.

**Empty bar feels silly — skip it?**  
Don’t. Empty bar *is* skill practice. Load comes after the cues stick.

**Do I need a number everywhere?**  
Only on The Bulk.

**What if my back rounds a little?**  
Stop, take weight off, and fix the setup and the brace. Film it.

**How does this relate to dumbbell / kettlebell?**  
Same five parts. Goblet Path is a good step before Squat Basics. A swing that sits the hips back helps Deadlift Basics.

**Can I mix sumo and conventional?**  
Pick one stance and stay with it for a while.
