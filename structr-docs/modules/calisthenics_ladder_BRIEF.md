## 1. What this is (and why skill practice)

Calisthenics is a ladder. Pull-up, then a harder pull. Push-up, then pike, then the wall. Squat, then one-leg steps. You earn the next rung when this one is clean and you still have reps left.

Structr frames that as **practice**: skill drills light, form honest, Bulk denser — same spine as kettlebell:

**Warm-up → Skill drills → Form → THE BULK → Cool-down**

**What we ship in this module**

| Template | Skill spine |
|----------|-------------|
| **Pull Ladder** | Hang, then shoulder blades, then rows and slow lowers, then a strict pull-up |
| **Push Ladder** | Push-up → pike / elevated → wall-oriented strength |
| **Squat Ladder** | Assisted squat → shrimp / step-downs → single-leg path |

> If it hurts in a sharp way, step off the bar. Film a set, or find a coach when you can.

## 2. Easy misses

### Pull ladder

**The idea:** Own the hang and the shoulder blades before you chase your chin over the bar. Slow lowers and rows build the middle rungs. Move up when the sets are clean and you still have something left.

**Common misses**

| Mistake | What it looks like | Better cue |
|---------|-------------------|------------|
| Skipping the hang | You jump straight to pull-up attempts | A dead hang and shoulder-blade pulls first, every session |
| A short pull | Your chin barely moves, then you bounce | Lower all the way. Chin clears the bar on the way up when you're ready |
| A swing instead of a pull | Your body swings and your arms hitch a ride | Keep the body quiet. Let the pull do the work |
| Ego advance | Harder variation before current is easy | 2 clean sessions with reserve before next rung |
| Only pull, never push | Rounded shoulders over time | Pair with Push Ladder days |

### Push ladder

**The idea:** Full push-ups before the fancy angles. Pike and wall work are skill and strength. Do them while your shoulders are fresh.

**Common misses**

| Mistake | What it looks like | Better cue |
|---------|-------------------|------------|
| Short push-ups | Your chest never gets near the floor | Slow, all the way down. Raise your hands if you need to. Keep the depth |
| Banana wall hold | Soft ribs, arched low back | Ribs down; glutes soft-squeeze; long line |
| Skipping wrists | Wrists scream mid-session | Wrist prep every push/inversion day |
| Practicing cooked | Handstand attempts after heavy push | Skill early, while fresh |
| Elbows flare, shoulders shrug | Shoulders climb toward your ears | Shoulders down. Elbows follow the path you meant |

### Squat ladder

**The idea:** Own a two-leg squat before the one-leg work. Use a hand, a hold, and step-downs. Build the rung you can stand back up from.

**Common misses**

| Mistake | What it looks like | Better cue |
|---------|-------------------|------------|
| Collapsing knee | Working knee caves | Knee tracks toes; hold a support lightly |
| Bouncing out | Momentum leaves the hole | Pause; own the bottom |
| Ignoring ankles | Heels lift forever | Elevate heels temporarily; keep practicing flat |
| Too much too soon | Max pistol attempts daily | Ladder rungs + reserve rule |

## 3. The three plans

### Pull Ladder · ~35–45 min

**Purpose:** Climb from hang toward a strict pull-up (and beyond) without thrashing elbows.  
**Who it’s for:** Beginners with a bar, returners rebuilding, anyone whose “pull day” is only curls.  
**Session shape**

| Phase | What you do |
|-------|-------------|
| Warm-up | Slow shoulder circles, shoulder-blade circles, a light hang |
| Skill drills | Scap pulls, inverted rows / band work |
| Form | Current rung @ quality (negatives / assisted / strict) |
| **THE BULK** | More reps of the rung you're on · **aim for 7–8** |
| Cool-down | Dead hang easy, doorway, breathe |

### Push Ladder · ~35–45 min

**Purpose:** Build push strength toward pike / wall-oriented work with wrists happy.  
**Who it’s for:** Floor push-up owners ready for harder angles; wall-curious without rushing freestanding.  
**Session shape**

| Phase | What you do |
|-------|-------------|
| Warm-up | Wrists, set the shoulder blades, easy push-ups |
| Skill drills | Pike holds, wall walks / chest-to-wall (as ready) |
| Form | Current push rung — quality |
| **THE BULK** | More push reps · **aim for 7–8** |
| Cool-down | Wrist stretches, child’s pose, breathe |

### Squat Ladder · ~35–45 min

**Purpose:** Own single-leg progressions without reckless pistol spam.  
**Who it’s for:** After bilateral squat feels honest (goblet or bodyweight).  
**Session shape**

| Phase | What you do |
|-------|-------------|
| Warm-up | Ankle rocks, hip openers, air squats |
| Skill drills | Assisted single-leg, step-downs, holds |
| Form | Current squat rung |
| **THE BULK** | More one-leg reps · **aim for 7–8** |
| Cool-down | 90/90, fold, breathe |

Open **Pull Ladder** first. A pull-up is the skill most people are missing.

## 4. How hard it felt

Same 1–10 number on The Bulk. Days with a lot of handstand or wall work can sit around **6–7**. Leave a little. Ladders pay you back tomorrow.

## 5. What you need

| Gear | Why |
|------|-----|
| Pull-up bar | Doorway or wall — the unlock for Pull Ladder |
| Floor space | Push / squat need clear mats |
| Optional bands | Help on a rung while the current one still feels doable |
| Optional parallettes | Wrist-friendlier push angles later |

Shoes you trust or barefoot for squat ladder. No fancy rings required day one.

## 6. FAQ

**Is this a CrossFit WOD?**  
These are ladders. The Bulk is more reps of the rung you're on.

**Which ladder first?**  
**Pull Ladder** if you lack a pull-up; otherwise rotate push/pull across the week.

**How do I know I can advance a rung?**  
Two sessions of clean sets with a little left in the tank — then step up.

**Do I need rings / parallettes?**  
A bar and the floor cover the three plans. Rings can wait.

**Handstand freestanding today?**  
A freestanding handstand comes later. Push Ladder builds the wall work first. Wider bar and wall skills live under **Bodyweight skills**.

**Relation to kettlebell / DB?**  
Same five parts. Strength from the bells helps. This is where the bodyweight steps live.
